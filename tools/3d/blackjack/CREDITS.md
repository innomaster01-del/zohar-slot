# Blackjack assets

- Dealer avatar: **Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library**, `Business_Male_04` (facial-blendshape version) and its motion-capture
  clips (idle, invite_sit, claphands, shrug, talk, listen_accept, wave) — MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020 Microsoft.
  https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox . Re-dressed as a casino dealer (recolored texture, added bow tie);
  dealing / flip / collect / pay / clear clips are baked here with two-bone IK over the idle mocap (bake.js).
- Table, cards, chips: built in code here (table.js, cards.js, chips.js). No third-party art.
- Money wheel host: **Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library**, `Business_Female_04` (facial-blendshape version) and its
  motion-capture clips (idle, wave, presentation, talk, claphands, cheer, shrug, laugh, touch hair) — MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2020 Microsoft. Restyled with tools/3d/host/recolor.py (red satin jacket, make-up); spin clip baked with IK.
